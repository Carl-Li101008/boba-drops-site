This site

Built from scratch for the Boba Drops workshop. What's actually implemented:

  • Dark / light theme with the choice saved in localStorage
  • Reading-progress bar that follows the scroll position
  • Scroll-reveal sections via IntersectionObserver
  • One-click copy of my email address, with inline feedback
  • Visit counter that stays on this device
  • Responsive from 320px up, with a prefers-reduced-motion fallback
